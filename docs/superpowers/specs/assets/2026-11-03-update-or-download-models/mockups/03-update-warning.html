The "Update ComfyUI" warning

The checkbox starts on, with a big red warning. Untick it: the warning changes to a calm message saying ComfyUI and torch stay as they are. The red note appears only when a selected workload needs a newer torch than the one installed. The torch versions in it are an example.

Content step, "Update existing ComfyUI"
Update ComfyUI

To the newest stable release, following your ComfyUI-Manager setting.

!

An update can destroy a working ComfyUI

If your ComfyUI works today, this is the moment to think twice. After an update:

  • Custom nodes may stop loading
  • Workflows you rely on may stop working
  • There's no automatic way back

Everything working? Untick this. You still get the workload's custom nodes and models.
